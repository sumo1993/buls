╔══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║                                                                  ║
║     HOW TO FIX YOUR NAME FROM "COMMITTEE" TO YOUR REAL NAME     ║
║                                                                  ║
╚══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝

┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  STEP 1: LOGIN                                                   │
└──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

    1. Open browser
    2. Go to: http://localhost:3000
    3. Click "Sign In"
    4. Enter your email and password
    5. Click "Sign In"

┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  STEP 2: GO TO SETTINGS                                          │
└──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

    Option A: Click "Settings" in the navigation menu

    Option B: Type in browser: http://localhost:3000/settings

┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  STEP 3: UPDATE YOUR NAME                                        │
└──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

    1. Look for section: "Personal Information"
    
    2. Find field: "Full Name *"
    
    3. You will see: [Committee          ]
    
    4. Clear it and type your real name:
       Example: [John Doe            ]
    
    5. Must include FIRST and LAST name!
    
       ✅ Valid:   "John Doe"
       ✅ Valid:   "Mary Jane Smith"
       ❌ Invalid: "John" (only one word)
       ❌ Invalid: "Committee" (placeholder)

┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  STEP 4: WAIT FOR AUTO-SAVE                                      │
└──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

    • Stop typing for 3 seconds
    
    • Look in top-right corner:
    
      💾 Saving...           ← Wait for this
      
      ✅ Saved 2 seconds ago ← Success!

┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  STEP 5: VERIFY YOUR NAME                                        │
└──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

    1. Go to "My Tickets" in the menu
    
    2. If you have tickets, click "View" on any ticket
    
    3. Your REAL NAME should now appear instead of "Committee"!

╔══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║                      IMPORTANT NOTES                             ║
╚══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝

  ⚠️  Your name MUST include both first AND last name
  
  ⚠️  Do NOT use: "Committee", "Admin", "Test", "User", "Member"
  
  ⚠️  Wait for "Saved ✓" before closing the page
  
  ⚠️  If you see an error, read it carefully and fix the issue

╔══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║                     COMMON ERRORS                                ║
╚══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝

  ❌ "Please enter your real full name, not a placeholder"
     → You typed "Committee" or another blocked word
     → Fix: Use your actual name
  
  ❌ "Please enter your full name (first and last name)"
     → You only entered one word
     → Fix: Add your last name too
  
  ❌ "Failed to save changes"
     → Internet connection issue
     → Fix: Check WiFi and try again
